The Mountain of Destiny

While I am still navigating the ascent and haven't conquered its peak just yet, my expeditions through the surrounding high-altitude valleys taught me the ultimate lesson in risk management. Up there, weather changes in minutes, and survival depends entirely on agile contingency planning.
